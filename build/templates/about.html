About the Readable English Bible

The Readable English Bible is an open-source Bible translation written in clear, simple English. It is made for new readers, young readers, people learning English, and anyone who wants the Bible to be easy to understand.

Open source

The text of this translation is open. You are free to read it, copy it, quote it, print it, share it, and use it in your own apps, websites, and ministries.

How it reads

We use short sentences and common words. Where a name or word has a special meaning in Hebrew or Greek, a short note at the bottom of the chapter explains it.

Work in progress

This is a draft translation and it is still being improved. If you find a mistake or have a suggestion, we would love to hear from you.
